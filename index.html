About

Built in El Paso, from the ground up.

I AM Real Estate started in 2017, when Gerardo "Jerry" Gonzalez and Hugo Gomez began buying, rehabbing and selling homes in El Paso. Jerry learned business at 12 in his family's tire shop; Hugo went from truck driver to investor. Together they grew into flipping, wholesaling, rentals and commercial property.

Our mission is simple: honest, professional real estate service tailored to each family, and neighborhoods that are better after we leave than before we arrived.
