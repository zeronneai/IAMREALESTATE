Text Message (SMS) Consent

I AM Real Estate is a trade name of Tesfaye Capital LLC, 3590 N Zaragoza Rd, Ste B103, El Paso, TX 79938. This page shows every way a customer can agree to receive text messages from us. We only text people who opted in, and consent is never a condition of any purchase or service.

What customers receive: replies to their inquiry about buying, selling or renting a home, appointment and showing confirmations and reminders, updates about properties they asked about, and payment reminders for current tenants and owner-financed buyers.
Frequency: message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.
Opt out: reply STOP at any time. Help: reply HELP or call (915) 206-2916.
Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing.

1. Website form (unchecked checkbox)

On our website, iamrealestate.vercel.app, every contact, offer and inquiry form has an optional checkbox for text messages. It is unchecked by default and the form can be sent without checking it. The checkbox text reads:

Optional: I agree to receive text messages from I AM Real Estate about my inquiry, appointments and properties. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Screenshot of the contact form at iamrealestate.vercel.app/#contact (the same checkbox appears on the sell, investor, property management and rental forms).

2. Customer texts us first

Our phone number is shown at the top of the website, in the contact section and in our listings:

(915) 206-2916

Customers can start a conversation by texting this number, or by texting START. Their first message is a request to talk with us by text. The first reply they receive is:

I AM Real Estate: You are now opted in to receive text messages about your inquiry, appointments and properties. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

3. Verbal consent during a phone call

Who asks: the I AM Real Estate team member on the call (agent or office staff).
When: during an inbound or scheduled call with a customer who is asking about buying, selling or renting a home, before any text is sent.
What they say (script):

"Would it be okay if we send you text messages from I AM Real Estate about your inquiry, appointments and the properties we talk about? Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. This is optional and not required for anything we do for you."

We only text the customer if they say yes. The team member then records in the customer's file the date, time, the phone number and the name of the person who collected the consent. The first text we send confirms the opt-in and includes STOP and HELP instructions.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to stop all texts; you will receive one confirmation: "You're unsubscribed from I AM Real Estate texts. Reply START to resubscribe." Reply HELP for help: "I AM Real Estate: call or text (915) 206-2916. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."
