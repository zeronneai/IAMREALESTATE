Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tesfaye Capital LLC, doing business as I AM Real Estate ("I AM Real Estate", "we", "us"), collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website, fill out a form, call or text us, or work with us to buy, sell, rent, finance or manage a property.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email and mailing address.
  • Property details you share: address, condition, mortgage or payment information you choose to provide, and your goals.
  • Messages and call notes when you call, text, email or chat with us.
  • Basic website data such as pages visited, browser type and approximate location, used to keep the site working and improve it.
  • Public records about real estate, such as county appraisal and recorded documents.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and give you an offer, analysis or proposal you asked for.
  • To schedule and remind you of appointments and showings.
  • To send updates about your inquiry, your property, your account, payments and listings that match what you are looking for.
  • To manage rentals and owner financed properties, including payment reminders and receipts.
  • To keep records and meet legal and regulatory requirements.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in, we send text messages from (915) 206-2916 about your inquiry, appointments, properties, payments and account updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase or service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver messages (for example, our messaging platform), and they may not use it for their own purposes.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide our services: with title companies, attorneys, lenders, loan servicers, inspectors and similar partners involved in your transaction (when you ask us to move forward), with service providers that host our website, systems and communications, and when required by law. The text messaging limits above always apply.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including access controls so only authorized team members can see your information.

Your choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message, or tell us by phone or email.
  • Calls and emails: ask us to stop contacting you and we will mark your record.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law.

Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Política de privacidad

Última actualización: 24 de noviembre de 2026

Esta política explica cómo Tesfaye Capital LLC, que opera como I AM Real Estate, recopila, usa y protege tu información cuando visitas nuestro sitio, llenas un formulario, nos llamas o escribes, o trabajas con nosotros para comprar, vender, rentar, financiar o administrar una propiedad.

Qué información recopilamos

Tus datos de contacto (nombre, teléfono, correo y dirección), los datos de la propiedad que compartes, tus mensajes y notas de llamadas, datos básicos de navegación del sitio y registros públicos de bienes raíces.

Para qué la usamos

Para contestarte, darte la oferta o análisis que pediste, agendar y recordarte citas, mandarte avisos sobre tu solicitud, propiedad, cuenta, pagos y casas que te interesen, administrar rentas y propiedades con financiamiento del dueño, y cumplir con la ley.

Mensajes de texto (SMS)

Si das tu consentimiento, te mandamos mensajes desde el (915) 206-2916 sobre tu solicitud, citas, propiedades, pagos y tu cuenta. La frecuencia varía. Pueden aplicar cargos por mensajes y datos. Responde STOP para dejar de recibirlos y HELP para ayuda. Aceptar los mensajes no es condición de compra ni de ningún servicio.

No compartimos tu número ni tu información móvil con terceros ni afiliados para fines de marketing o promoción. Los datos y el consentimiento para recibir mensajes no se comparten con terceros, salvo con los proveedores que nos ayudan a enviar los mensajes, que no pueden usarlos para otros fines.

Con quién la compartimos

No vendemos tu información. Solo la compartimos con quienes participan en tu operación cuando nos pides avanzar (compañía de título, abogados, prestamistas, inspectores), con los proveedores de nuestro sitio y sistemas, y cuando la ley lo exige.

Tus opciones

Puedes pedirnos dejar de contactarte, ver, corregir o borrar tu información (salvo lo que la ley nos pida guardar).
